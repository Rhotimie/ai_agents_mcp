# Banoffee Pie Recipe

A foolproof, delicious, no-bake classic British dessert featuring a buttery biscuit base, rich homemade caramel, fresh bananas, whipped cream, and dark chocolate shavings. 

* **Prep Time:** 30 minutes
* **Chilling Time:** 1 hour
* **Total Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes
* **Servings:** 8 People

---

## Ingredients

### For the Base:
* **200 g (11 oz)** Digestive biscuits (crushed) — *Can substitute with Graham Crackers if in the USA*
* **100 g (3.5 oz)** Unsalted butter (melted)
* **½ tsp** Salt

### For the Caramel:
* **80 g** Unsalted butter
* **80 g** Dark brown sugar
* **397 g (14 oz)** Can of sweetened condensed milk *(Do NOT use evaporated milk)*

### For the Topping:
* **2** Ripe bananas (peeled and sliced)
* **300 ml (1.25 cups)** Double cream (heavy cream for US/CAN, thickened cream for AUS) — *Whipped*
* **10 g (3.5 oz)** Dark chocolate (for shavings/curls)

### Optional Additions:
* **2 tsp** Vanilla extract (added to the cream)
* **2 tbsp** Icing sugar / powdered sugar (added to the cream)
* A squeeze of lemon juice (tossed with banana slices to prevent browning)

---

## Instructions

1. **Make the Base:** 
   * Mix the crushed digestive biscuits with the melted butter and salt until well combined.
   * Press the mixture firmly into a 23cm removable base flan tin using the back of a spoon. 
   * Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes if you have time.

2. **Make the Caramel:** 
   * Place the 80g butter and dark brown sugar into a small non-stick saucepan over low heat. Stir continuously until melted and the sugar dissolves into a liquid.
   * Add the condensed milk. 
   * Turn the heat to medium, continue stirring, and let the caramel bubble rapidly for about 1 minute until thick and golden. *(Note: Cook a minute longer if needed to ensure a thick, fudgy set).*

3. **Add the Bottom Layer:** 
   * Pour the warm caramel evenly onto your chilled biscuit base and smooth it out. 
   * Return to the fridge and chill for a minimum of 1 hour to set.

4. **Layer It Up:** 
   * Top the set caramel layer with sliced bananas.
   * Spoon the whipped cream (whipped to soft, floppy peaks, optionally mixed with vanilla extract and icing sugar) evenly over the bananas.

5. **Optional Topping & Serve:** 
   * Finish with dark chocolate shavings (use a vegetable peeler to create curls) or a light dusting of cocoa powder. 
   * Slice, serve, and enjoy!

---

## Recipe Tips & Storage
* **Preventing Brown Bananas:** Toss the banana slices in a little lemon juice before placing them on the pie to keep them fresh and add a nice tang.
* **Storage:** Keep leftovers in the fridge for up to 3 days. Freezing is not recommended for this dessert.
